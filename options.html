Blocked words

A word only counts when it stands on its own, so blocking “ass” leaves “pass” and “provpass” alone. It's still caught when it's split up: “b.a.l.l”, “b_all”, “b?all” and “b all” are all treated as “ball”.

Click ≈ on a word to also catch misspellings, like “cuomshot” for “cumshot”. On words under 5 letters it only catches repeated letters, like “nsfww” for “nsfw”, because changing a letter would turn “porn” into “pork” or “born”.

Word packs

Tick the lists you want and add them in one go. Plurals and the usual tricks are caught automatically, and words marked ≈ also catch misspellings: they were checked against the most common English and Swedish words so everyday searches stay open. You can remove any single word later.

      Help searches

      Searches for help get through even though they contain a blocked word, so “how to quit porn” stays open. Any other blocked word in the same search still closes the tab. This list is fixed and can’t be added to, so it can never become a way around your blocked words.
