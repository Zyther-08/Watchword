# Watchword privacy policy

*Effective 13 October 2026*

Watchword is a free browser extension by the Watchword Project. It closes the tab when you search for a word you've blocked. This page explains exactly what it does with your information. The short version: **nothing leaves your computer.**

## What Watchword collects

Nothing. Watchword has no server, no account and no analytics. It makes no network requests of its own, contains no ads and no third-party code, and never loads code from the internet.

## What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser's extension storage, on your own computer only:

- your blocked words and blocked sites
- your settings (for example SafeSearch, typing detection and image search)
- the lock, if you set one: either the length of the typing challenge, or a password stored only as a salted hash (the password itself is never saved)
- the end time of a 10-minute image search pass, while one is running

They are not synced to other devices or sent anywhere. Removing Watchword from your browser deletes them.

## What Watchword looks at

To do its job, Watchword checks two things against your blocked words, on your own computer:

- the search term in the address of pages you open (for example, the words after `?q=` on a search results page)
- the text you type into search boxes on websites

Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. Watchword doesn't read the rest of the pages you visit.

## SafeSearch

When SafeSearch is on, Watchword adds the SafeSearch setting to your searches on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo, and Restricted Mode to YouTube. It does this with fixed browser rules. It doesn't read, log or change your requests in any other way.

## Device guides

The settings page has guides for protecting other devices with Cloudflare's free family filter. Watchword sends nothing to Cloudflare. If you choose to follow a guide, your device's DNS lookups go to Cloudflare under [Cloudflare's own resolver privacy commitments](https://developers.cloudflare.com/1.1.1.1/privacy/public-dns-resolver/).

## Children

Watchword is suitable for family computers. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Any change that would collect data would need a new version of the extension, and stores show that before updating.
